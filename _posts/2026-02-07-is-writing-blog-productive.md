*2026/02/07*

*Estimated Reading Time : 3-4min*

In an age when AI can write great articles within seconds, it can truely feel like creating any kind of content is a waste of time. So if you are reading this, you may be thinking about starting something of your own but feel discouraged by the current circumstances. While I was trying to decide whether to commit or not, I found three criteria that allowed me to feel confident about my decision.

The Three Criteria:

1.  Can the time spent on writing be better used on another activity?
2.  Are you trying to create an audience?
3.  Does what you want to say have to be communicated through lengthy written text?

The first criterion is self-explanatory. Even though there are many services that allow anyone to start blogging within 10 minutes, writing each article takes time. If you are trying to build a successful website, you will need to maintain your motivation for more than a year. Everything requires effort, and blogging is no exception. If you are only in it for the money, do it only if you are unemployed or working below minimum wage. Otherwise, you will reach your financial [goals](/2026/02/13/goal-setting/) faster by working part-time today than by blogging for a year.

The second criterion can also be satisfied through other media. You may have seen many influencers on YouTube, TikTok, Bluesky, and so on. However, the advantage of written text is that search and trending algorithms do not change as drastically as they do on other platforms. In addition, the higher barrier to entry for traffic means you are more likely to gain loyal readers who provide higher-quality feedback. Especially on TikTok, even though content spreads easily, it is difficult to receive recognition because content is automatically curated rather than hand-picked by users. Comments on blogs are also more demanding than those on social media, which helps filter out half-baked [criticism](/2026/05/10/devils-advocate/).

The final criterion can help you reconsider alternatives. As with any goal, there is more than one way to achieve it. Nowadays, there are short- and long-form videos, podcasts, tweets, GitHub repositories, and so on. Tools can surprisingly be the deciding factor in a project’s success or failure, so you and I need to think carefully about how to share our content. Each method definitely has its own pros and cons, so I recommend writing them down and comparing them critically. Even after objective analysis, if lengthy text still seems like the best option, then start writing.

As a final caution, some people may still find it difficult to decide whether to begin blogging because the answers to all three questions are not clearly “yes” or “no.” In such cases, I suggest a small calculation. Assign a weight to each criterion based on its importance: 3 points for the most important, 2 for the middle, and 1 for the least important. Then multiply each score by 1 or −1 depending on whether the answer is “yes” or “no,” and add up the results. If the total is greater than 3, you’ve found your calling. If it’s less, you may be better off being productive elsewhere.

Thanks for reading, and don’t forget to be productive!
