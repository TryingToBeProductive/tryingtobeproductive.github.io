We live in a time when anyone in the world can interrupt our flow from anywhere, as long as our phones have a signal. As most of us have experienced, once the flow is disrupted, it can take quite a while to regain it. However, some messages are important, and if left unchecked they may cause minor headaches later. Notifications need to be approached carefully, or they may harm our productivity.

The most basic advice you hear when confronted with this problem is to turn off most notifications and set a specific time block during the day to scan through them, reply when necessary, or add tasks if needed. From my experience, if you actually follow this advice, it does solve the issue with relatively little sacrifice. Especially when you [block](/2026/02/24/blocking-distractions/) notifications from even appearing on your screen and give yourself a comfortable amount of time to deal with each message or request, your performance is protected while communication breakdowns are prevented.

Of course, there may be some frustration from the receiving end of such measures. I have experienced this as a receiving end myself, and what surprised me was that people, myself included, began adjusting their messaging habits to align with those of so-called “hard-to-reach” individuals. Even if you have not told others that your availability is restricted, your contacts will sense it in a short time. If your weekly schedule is somewhat predictable, acquaintances will try to call during times when you are more likely to pick up in order to save their own time and effort. They may also send emails earlier and wait longer for replies so their workflow is not interrupted.

But you might wonder, “What about [emergencies](/2026/03/20/dealing-with-system-breakdowns/)?” If you explore the settings of most communication apps, you will find filters that allow immediate notifications from specific people in your life who are most likely to contact you when something urgent happens. For family members, you could even have everyone use a separate app. This creates a platform dedicated to your household while ensuring that family members can respond quickly if you need help.

The [default](/2026/03/18/questioning-default/) setting of our digital landscape is constant availability. However, focus requires isolation, and controlling notifications is a first step toward achieving that. Once you establish a working system, your productivity will be safeguarded. Furthermore, a clear hierarchy of communication channels will allow you to respond to genuine crises more effectively.
